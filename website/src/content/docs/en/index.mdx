## Features

<CardGrid stagger>
	<Card title="Earphone controls" icon="setting">
		Battery and firmware version, ANC and transparency levels, game mode, EQ, touch bindings, wear detection, and multipoint. What you see depends on the model and firmware.
	</Card>
	<Card title="HyperOS integration" icon="star">
		Through the Xposed module: system island, focus notifications, and Fusion Device Center. Under module control, the system Bluetooth process connects to the earphones without OpenRose open.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Two UI styles" icon="laptop">
		Miuix and Material 3 Expressive; switching keeps the earphones connected. Runs on Android, and on the Linux desktop with control through BlueZ.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Local and open source" icon="open-book">
		Talks to the earphones directly over local Bluetooth; no account needed. Licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
