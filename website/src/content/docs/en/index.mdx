## Features

<CardGrid stagger>
	<Card title="HyperOS Deep Integration" icon="star">
		Connects with Super Island, Focus notifications, and Convergence Device Center. Supports direct management from the system Bluetooth process via Xposed.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Dual UI & Cross-Platform" icon="laptop">
		Powered by Kotlin & Compose Multiplatform, offering Miuix and Material 3 Expressive UI themes across Android and Linux (BlueZ).
	</Card>
	<Card title="Full Device Control" icon="setting">
		Comprehensive controls for ANC/transparency levels, gaming low-latency mode, 10-band custom EQ, gesture mappings, and dual-device connection.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Local & Open Source" icon="open-book">
		No official account required. All operations communicate directly via local Bluetooth Low Energy without background tracking.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
